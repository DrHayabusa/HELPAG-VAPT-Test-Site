About us

Forwarding, brokerage and the unglamorous parts in between

Meridian Freight Solutions was founded in Rotterdam in 1998 as a customs brokerage. We added groupage in 2004, our first sea consolidation service in 2009, and intermodal rail in 2017. We employ 340 people across eleven depots.

How we work

Every account has a named operations contact and an escalation path that ends with a person, not a queue. Customs declarations are filed by our own licensed brokers rather than subcontracted.

Compliance

We hold AEO-F authorisation in the Netherlands and Germany, and operate an ISO 9001-certified quality system. Our security and data protection posture is reviewed annually by an external assessor.
